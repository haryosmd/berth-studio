It protects you as a contributor, the maintainer and every user of Berth Studio. You keep the copyright to your contribution. You sign once, and it covers all your future contributions to Berth Studio.

You accept this agreement by commenting `I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA` on your first pull request, when the CLA Assistant bot asks for it.
